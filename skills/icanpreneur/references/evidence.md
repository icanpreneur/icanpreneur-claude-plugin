# Evidence rules

Icanpreneur exists to help people decide from customer evidence. The tools hold three kinds of material that look alike but are not equal. Keep them apart in everything you say.

## What marks each kind

| Material                         | How to tell                                                                                                                             | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Interview with a real respondent | `synthetic: false`, `imported: false`                                                                                                   | A real conversation held on the platform (AI-led or host-led).                                                                                                                                    |
| Imported interview               | `synthetic: false`, `imported: true`                                                                                                    | A supplied transcript marked as imported, not independently verified as an authentic real interview. Attribute provenance to the user. It may lack speaker roles, timestamps and script coverage. |
| Synthetic interview              | `synthetic: true`                                                                                                                       | Generated by Icanpreneur AI. A simulation.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Contact                          | `synthetic` on the contact                                                                                                              | `false` for people someone saved; `true` for generated profiles.                                                                                                                                  |
| Insight or quality source        | `synthetic` on each entry in `contributingInsights` and `contributingRecommendations`                                                   | Tells you whether a finding rests on real or simulated interviews.                                                                                                                                |
| Buyer Persona answer             | Always simulated                                                                                                                        | A perspective generated by the persona service from the persona's research.                                                                                                                       |
| Buyer Persona record             | **No flag.** `interviewCount` mixes real and synthetic. `status: Complete` can also describe an empty initial persona; inspect content. | Built from eligible analyzed interviews in its research. The tools do not show which interviews a given revision used.                                                                            |

## Finding the evidence in a persona's source research

1. `get_buyer_persona` returns the persona's `researchId` and `researchName`. `get_research` takes that `researchId` as its input and returns the research's own `id`.
2. `list_interviews` with `researchId`, `stage: "Completed"`, `take: 100`. Keep paging with `skip` until you have read as many as `totalCount`. Never report a partial page as a total.
3. Count by `synthetic` and `imported`. Also note interviews not yet completed (`get_research` shows `notStartedCount` and `inProgressCount`).
4. State it in one line: "The source research currently contains 8 completed interviews: 3 real (1 imported) and 5 synthetic." If every interview is synthetic, say that first.

This describes the research's membership **now**, not the interview set used by a particular profile or answer. `buyerPersonaVersion` identifies the revision at conversation creation; `usesLatestPersona: false` means the persona record has changed since then. It does not establish an immutable historical profile or evidence snapshot. Say that the conversation began at an earlier revision and the counts describe the research today.

An interview can belong to more than one research project. When you combine totals across research projects, de-duplicate by interview ID.

Do this once per persona per conversation. It is free but it can take several calls for a large research. If the user wants the evidence behind a particular reaction, go further: read the research insights and their contributing interviews.

## Rules

1. **Synthetic is not validation.** Never describe synthetic-only results as validated, confirmed, proven, or as customer demand. Use "simulated", "hypothesis", "worth testing". Real interviews can support "customers said". Say how many.
2. **Persona answers are simulated perspectives.** They show how the persona, as built from the research, would likely react. They do not show what customers will do. After a persona answer, name what is uncertain and what a real customer test would need to show.
3. **Quote only what a tool returned.** A `quote` can be null. Leave it null. Do not write a plausible one. A persona's `mainQuote` is not a verified quote from a real customer. Never present it as a testimonial.
4. **Interviewer words are not respondent evidence.** `get_interview_transcript` returns a `transcript` string: structured XML for standard interviews and extracted text for imports. Use explicit participant/speaker labels in the returned content; do not assume the tool returns typed message objects. Imported transcripts may have no roles. Do not invent speakers.
5. **Missing stays missing.** Null contact fields, null timestamps and absent picture stay null. Do not fill them with guesses, and do not infer a person's attributes.
6. **Never record invented people as real.** `create_contact` saves `synthetic: false` whatever you enter in `bio`. Do not use it for fictional respondents, simulated respondents, or profiles modeled on a real person. Use `generate_synthetic_contacts` for synthetic profiles. If the user wants a profile modeled on a real person, explain that the tools can store a real person or generate a synthetic profile, but not a hand-written synthetic one, and ask how they want to proceed. Never attribute a simulated answer to the real person.
7. **Scores measure interview execution.** Quality is typically a score out of 100 and coverage a percentage of script lines covered. Report values with their explanation; they do not measure business validity or evidence confidence. `percentageImprovement` is an estimated prioritization value, not a measured percentage.
8. **Traction is quantitative evidence of demand** (for example customer acquisition or a growing user base). Interviews, personas and synthetic results are not traction.
9. **Cite freshness.** When reporting analysis, give `updatedOn`. If `status` is `Pending`, say results may not include the latest interviews.
10. **Generated assets inherit their source.** Positioning, landing pages and decks capture the persona at the time they were generated. If that persona rested on synthetic interviews, the asset is a hypothesis, and you should say so when you hand it over.

## Wording

| Avoid                                    | Use                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Customers want X." (synthetic only)     | "The simulated interviews suggest X. No real customer has said this yet."                                                              |
| "The persona confirmed the price works." | "The persona, a simulated view, reacted well to the price. Test it with real buyers."                                                  |
| "Validated by research."                 | "Supported by 4 real interviews; 3 of 4 mentioned it."                                                                                 |
| "A customer said: …" (from `mainQuote`)  | "The persona profile's representative quote reads: …"                                                                                  |
| "The business is 72% validated."         | "Interview quality scores 72/100, with this note: …" (report the returned value and note; do not invent a business-validity threshold) |

Keep the caveat proportional. State it clearly once where it applies. Do not repeat it after every sentence.
