# Mentoring brief (one per startup)

Use the same four parts for every team, so a mentor can read them side by side. Keep each brief to what the workspace shows. Mark unknowns as unknown.

```markdown
## <Startup name>

**Strongest evidence:** <the best-supported finding, with the count of real or imported interviews, a short quote and the interview name where available; leave missing ones missing. If there is none, say that no real customer evidence is saved.>
**Biggest untested assumption:** <the assumption that matters most if wrong, upstream first, and why it matters.>
**Questions for the mentor:**

1. <A question that tests whether the team's segment or problem is clear enough.>
2. <A question about the gap between what they believe and what the evidence says.>
3. <A question about the next test and who they will talk to.>

**Next milestone:** <one concrete step that closes the gap, such as a research type, segment and who to interview.>
**Evidence basis:** <N completed interviews: X real (Z imported), Y synthetic; analysis as of <updatedOn>. Canvas items are hypotheses.>
```

Rules:

- Write the three questions from this team's own Canvas and findings, not generic coaching questions.
- The strongest evidence can be "none yet". Do not fill the space with synthetic results presented as customer evidence.
- Do not compare this team with another inside the brief.
- Use one brief per startup, and the same criteria for all.
