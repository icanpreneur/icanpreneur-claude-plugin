# Icanpreneur for Claude

Bring your Icanpreneur customer research and Buyer Personas into Claude.

Use the plugin to:

- Get Buyer Persona feedback on pricing, messaging, and product ideas.
- Prepare customer research and analyze interview findings.
- Review assumptions and decide what to test next.
- Create positioning, landing-page copy, and sales decks.

The plugin includes Icanpreneur skills and connects Claude to the
Icanpreneur platform through its hosted MCP server.
